Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Luiz Torribio ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the Level Up: Self Tracker app ("the App").

Information We Collect

The App stores all personal data — including habits, journal entries, workout logs, mood, sleep, goals, and books — locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit this data to any server.

Premium Subscriptions

If you purchase a subscription, payment is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information.

AI Coach Feature

If you use the AI Coach (premium feature), a summary of your in-app data is sent to Anthropic's API to generate a personalized message. This data is not stored by us.

Third-Party Services

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat may collect anonymous usage data. See their privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.

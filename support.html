FAQ

Common Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel at any time directly through Apple:

iPhone Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → tap Level Up: Self Tracker → tap Cancel Subscription.

Your access continues until the end of the current billing period. No refunds are issued for unused time, per Apple's standard policy.

How is my daily score calculated?

Your daily score is a 0–100 composite across 7 lifestyle pillars:

Habits — 25 pts  ·  Workout — 20 pts  ·  Sleep — 15 pts  ·  Mood — 10 pts  ·  Energy — 10 pts  ·  Reading — 10 pts  ·  Mindfulness — 10 pts

Each pillar is scaled based on the quantity or quality you log that day. The score resets every midnight — yesterday's score is saved in your history.

How do I reset my app data?

To reset all data and start fresh:

From the home screen, tap the gear icon (Settings) in the top right corner → scroll to the bottom → tap Reset All Data → confirm.

Warning: This permanently deletes all your habits, logs, workout history, and streak data. This action cannot be undone.
